## 🔍 IMMEDIATE DIAGNOSTIC ACTIONS (Do Today)

### 1. Google Search Console Audit (30 minutes)
**Check these sections in GSC:**

#### Security & Manual Actions
- Look for any red warnings
- Check for "Unnatural links" or "Thin content" penalties
- **If you see manual actions, this is your problem**

#### Experience → Core Web Vitals
- Check for "Poor" or "Needs Improvement" ratings
- Look for LCP, FID, or CLS issues
- **Poor Core Web Vitals can cause traffic drops**

#### Coverage Report
- Check indexed vs. submitted pages
- Look for "Error" or "Excluded" pages
- **This shows if Google stopped indexing your pages**

#### Experience → Mobile Usability
- Check for mobile-specific errors
- Look for usability issues
- **Mobile problems can hurt rankings**

### 2. External Testing (15 minutes)
