## 🚨 IMPORTANT NOTES

### Do's
- Focus on quality over quantity
- Build relationships, not just links
- Create valuable content that naturally attracts links
- Diversify anchor text naturally
- Monitor and disavow toxic links

### Don'ts
- Don't buy links or use link farms
- Don't use exact match anchor text excessively
- Don't ignore link quality for quantity
- Don't spam website owners
- Don't ignore Google's guidelines

---

**Priority**: Start with directory submissions and easy wins, then move to content creation and outreach for higher-quality backlinks.
